Task Tracker

by ArtivicoLab

A dark-mode task tracker that lives in your own Google Sheet.

Task Tracker is a phone-first web app that helps you organize tasks, recurring routines, and who's doing what. It opens in any browser on your phone, tablet, or computer, works offline, and can sync your data to a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive so you always own a backup.

What you can do

  • Plan one-off and recurring tasks with priorities, status, and due dates
  • Assign tasks to team or household members with quick-pick chips
  • See live charts: status, category, priority, activity timeline, and who's carrying what
  • See everything on a month, week, and day calendar that fills every date
  • Quick-capture: type a task straight into any calendar day

How Task Tracker uses your Google account

Connecting Google is optional. When you choose to connect, Task Tracker requests only the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. This limits access to the single spreadsheet the app creates for you, and nothing else in your Drive.

We use that access for one purpose: to save and sync your planner to that spreadsheet so it is backed up and available across your devices. Your sign-in stays in your browser and talks to Google directly from your device. ArtivicoLab operates no server and never receives, stores, or sells your data. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Your data stays yours

Everything works offline on your device first. If you connect Google, your data lives in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive that you can open, export, or delete any time. There is no subscription, and connecting to Google Sheets is free.

Frequently asked questions

Can Task Tracker see my other Google files or spreadsheets?
No. Task Tracker uses the Google "drive.file" permission, which lets it access only the single spreadsheet it creates for you. It cannot see, open, or list anything else in your Google Drive.

Where is my data stored?
On your device first, and in one spreadsheet in your own Google Drive once you connect Google. There is no server of ours in between.

Does ArtivicoLab have access to my data?
No. We run no server and never receive your data. Your planner talks to Google directly from your browser, using your own Google account.

Is there a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase with no monthly fees, and connecting to Google Sheets is free.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything works offline on your device and syncs to your sheet when you are back online.

What devices does it work on?
Any device with a modern browser: phone, tablet, or computer. You can also install it to your home screen like an app.

What happens if I disconnect or stop using it?
Your data stays in your own Google Sheet. Disconnecting only unlinks the app; it never deletes your sheet. You can open, export, or delete that sheet yourself any time.
